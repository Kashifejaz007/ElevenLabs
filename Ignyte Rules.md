# Ignyte: What We Need From You

## STAGE 1 · THE IDEA CANVAS · DUE 23 SEPTEMBER

Stage 1 submissions are made on the ElevenLabs Idea Canvas, the five-page template issued with this brief. The canvas is the submission.

*Submissions not made on the canvas are not reviewed.* Decks, documents, videos and attachments submitted in place of the canvas are not assessed.

Fourteen boxes across three pages. Each box states its word limit and the criterion it is scored against:

### 01 · The Opportunity (boxes A–E)
* Submission details
* The idea in one line
* What breaks today
* Today’s baseline
* Who buys this

### 02 · The Agent (boxes F–I)
* The call flow in five steps
* The ElevenLabs components you will use and why
* How it integrates
* A six-row guardrail table

### 03 · The Case (boxes J–N)
* Success metrics
* Risks
* What will be working by 14 October
* Your team
* Proof of build

---

## How the canvas is marked

* **Word limits are enforced.** Every box states a word limit. Text beyond the limit is not assessed.
* **Boxes D and J are cross-checked.** The baseline figures in box D must be the figures the KPIs in box J are measured against.
* **Box G is assessed on selection, not coverage.** Tick the components the use case requires and give reasons. Selecting every component without justification scores lower than a smaller, reasoned selection.
* **Box I requires mechanisms.** Each guardrail row must state how the design enforces the requirement. Statements of intent are not scored.
* **Box N requires a working link.** A live product, repository or previously deployed demo. Submissions without a working link score zero for this box.

**One canvas, one use case, one team.** Multiple entries from the same team are not accepted.
